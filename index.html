Eliminating the Middleman Inefficiency

How CropNex solves Problem Statement 26033: Reducing supply chain intermediaries so farmers earn more while buyers pay fair market rates.

The Problem: Traditional Supply Chain

5-6 layers of intermediaries extract value before produce reaches consumer.

Farmer ➔ Trader ➔ Wholesaler ➔ Distributor ➔ Retailer ➔ Consumer
  • Lower farmer margin (only 25-35% price realization)
  • Higher consumer price (200-300% retail markup)
  • Information gaps & mandi price opacity
  • High logistics wastage and delayed transit

The Solution: CropNex

Direct 3-tier model powered by AI price intelligence and smart freight.

Farmer ➔ CropNex ➔ Buyer
  • Better farmer margins (65-80% direct realization)
  • Transparent, fair wholesale & retail pricing
  • Direct verified buyers, institutions & FPOs
  • Smart route optimization & shared freight pooling
