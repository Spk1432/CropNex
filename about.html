Direct Agricultural Commerce, Empowering Indian Farmers

CropNex connects verified farmers directly with wholesale buyers, retailers, and food processors—ensuring fair farm-gate pricing, complete harvest traceability with scannable barcodes, and rapid cold-chain fulfillment.

Zero Middlemen

By removing multi-layered commission agents and adatyas, CropNex enables farmers to sell at true wholesale rates, yielding up to 35% higher direct earnings while reducing buyer procurement costs.

Harvest & Barcode Traceability

Every produce batch is encoded with its exact harvesting date, product life span rating, farm origin, and a scannable barcode verifying farm-direct authenticity and fresh stock quality.

AI Mandi Price Forecasting

Intelligent statistical models track 12 major Maharashtra APMC mandis (Lasalgaon, Pimpalgaon, Gultekdi Pune, Solapur) to project 15-day wholesale rate trends, preventing distress selling.

Shared Cold-Van Logistics

Smart multi-stop route pooling groups farm produce dispatches from agricultural corridors into shared cold-storage vans, slashing freight expenses by 21% and ensuring delivery within 24 hours.
